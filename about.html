ABOUT

Who We Are

BORN FROM
OBSESSION

Autobahn Haus was founded on a single belief: German automobiles represent the highest achievement in human engineering. Not just transportation — philosophy cast in steel, aluminium, and carbon fibre.

We are a dedicated resource for enthusiasts, collectors, and curious minds who want to understand what makes Porsche, Mercedes-Benz, Audi, and BMW truly exceptional.

Every model we feature has been selected for its engineering significance, its driving character, and its place in the lineage of one of the great automotive nations on earth.

A HISTORY OF
SPEED

1886

The First Automobile

Karl Benz patents the Benz Patent-Motorwagen — the world's first petrol-powered automobile.

1916

BMW Founded

Bayerische Motoren Werke established in Munich, beginning a century of precision motor engineering.

1931

Porsche Established

Ferdinand Porsche founds his engineering consultancy in Stuttgart — the seed that became the world's most successful sports car brand.

1963

Porsche 911 Unveiled

Frankfurt Motor Show witnesses the birth of the most iconic sports car ever made.

1984

Audi Quattro Dominates

Audi's quattro all-wheel-drive system revolutionises rally racing and road cars forever.

2024

Autobahn Haus Launches

We open our showroom — a celebration of everything Germany has contributed to the automotive world.

The Person Behind It

ABOUT
ME

I'm a lifelong car enthusiast who grew up with a poster of a Porsche 911 on my wall and a father who drove a BMW E30. German engineering has never just been about getting from A to B — for me, it's an obsession with craft, physics, and the pursuit of perfection.

After years of collecting, driving, and writing about German performance cars, I built Autobahn Haus as the definitive online resource for people who feel the same way.

I believe the best car is one that makes you feel something every single time you turn the key. German cars do that better than anyone else on earth.
